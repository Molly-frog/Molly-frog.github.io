About Us

Our Vision

The KoiBots aim to exemplify FIRST core values by becoming a role model in our school district and community, bringing students access to unique STEAM experiences.

Our Mission

The KoiBots' mission is to ensure that each student who graduates from the team leaves with tangible skills they can apply to the 21st-century workforce.

Who We Are

We are a student-led FIRST® Robotics team based out of the Grace Hopper Center. We cover all things STEM, from programming to CAD, from electrical to mechanical. We also have a spirited marketing and outreach department.
