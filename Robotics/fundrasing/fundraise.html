Why Us

 The ACC KoiBots are passionate about everything we do. We strive to establish ourselves as a fun, friendly, and enthusiastic team in robotics and as members of our school and community. However, we can only go so far without proper funding, and we need your support to reach our goals.

 By sponsoring us, you would support STEM-minded, robotics- loving students. Your investment would not only support us within the competition but would help us expand the STEM education of all of our students. Thank you for your time and consideration.


Best Regards,
The Koibots
